Roofing in Santa Barbara, CA

Coastal moisture, salt air, and strong sun punish flashings, fasteners, and underlayment. We specify materials and details suited to hillside and beach-adjacent homes—not inland defaults.

Homes along the South Coast

From Spanish tile to modern low-profile shingles, Santa Barbara architecture varies block by block. We align with your home’s style and any historic or design review requirements when they apply.

Repairs before they spread

Small leaks at chimneys, skylights, or wall junctions can soak sheathing fast. Our repair visits focus on stopping water entry and showing photos of what we found.

Full replacement

When it is time for a full replacement, we plan tear-off, dry-in, and inspections so your home stays protected between phases—especially during unpredictable weather windows.

Commercial & low-slope

Retail and mixed-use often need flat or low-slope expertise. Larger portfolios can use our HOA & commercial approach for phased work and clear reporting.
