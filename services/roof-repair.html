Roof repair

Targeted fixes for leaks, wind damage, failed flashing, and wear— with straight answers on whether repair or replacement is the smarter move.

Leak investigation

Water stains and drips do not always show where the roof failed. We trace entry points, check penetrations and valleys, and address the cause—not just the symptom.

Storms & emergencies

High winds and driving rain can lift flashing and shingles. We prioritize temporary protection when needed, then permanent repairs that match your existing system.

Repair vs. replace

If a roof is near end of life or repairs would repeat, we will tell you. Our goal is the right investment for your situation—not a quick patch that fails next season.
