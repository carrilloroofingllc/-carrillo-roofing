More services we offer

Pulled from the same scope of work customers know us for on carrillo-roofingllc.com—tell us what you need on the estimate form or by phone.

  • Metal roofing — durable, efficient installs in styles that suit coastal and inland homes.
  • Concrete & clay tile — new tile roofs and restorations, including tile lift & reset where appropriate.
  • Asphalt shingles — high-quality architectural systems with clean valleys and flashing details.
  • Single-ply & torch-down — commercial-grade low-slope solutions (see also flat roofing).
  • Gutters — installation, repair, and maintenance to move water away from your foundation.
  • Roof inspections — documented assessments for peace of mind, real estate, or storm follow-up.
  • Dry rot repair — structural repairs tied to moisture intrusion we discover during roofing work.
  • Soft-wash roof cleaning — gentle cleaning for algae, moss, and staining without aggressive pressure.
  • DaVinci composite slate & shake — premium composite profiles with strong curb appeal and impact resistance.

Warranty work & manufacturer certifications

Carrillo Roofing LLC installs to manufacturer standards across residential and commercial systems, including CertainTeed SELECT ShingleMaster™, GAF residential, GAF commercial, Elevate, ASC, and Mule-Hide product lines. If you need warranty service or have inspection paperwork, bring it to your estimate and we will walk through coverage and next steps.

Warranty work

We handle warranty-related repairs and documentation when your system qualifies—whether that is workmanship coverage on our installs or manufacturer-backed protection on eligible materials.

Certified manufacturer systems

We install certified systems from CertainTeed, GAF (residential and commercial), Elevate, ASC, and Mule-Hide according to each manufacturer program's requirements. That helps with registration, inspection compliance, and stronger manufacturer-backed coverage where your scope and materials qualify.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about estimates, coverage, and how we work.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Contact us for a free quote with no pressure. We review your roof, explain options, and provide clear pricing.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Carrillo Roofing LLC is licensed in California (CSLB #1123440) and carries insurance for your protection.

What warranties do you offer?

We stand behind our work with strong workmanship coverage and manufacturer-backed warranties on qualifying systems. Details depend on materials and scope—we cover this on every proposal.

How long does a typical roof replacement take?

Timeline depends on size, materials, weather, and scope. Many residential projects take a few days to a week. We give you a realistic schedule before work begins.

Do you work with HOAs and permits?

Yes. We are experienced with HOA requirements, documentation, and local permitting so your project stays compliant.

What areas do you serve?

Ventura, Santa Barbara, and Los Angeles Counties—including Oxnard, Ventura, Camarillo, Thousand Oaks, Moorpark, Ojai, Malibu, Simi Valley, Santa Barbara, and nearby cities. Ask if you are on the edge of our service area.

Can you provide references from past clients?

Yes. We can provide references from past clients. You can also read public reviews on Google and Yelp. If you would like to speak with specific past clients, ask us and we will coordinate when possible.

What sets you apart from other contractors?

We distinguish ourselves through attention to detail, a dedication to quality, and a personalized approach. Client communication comes first so your project stays on track from estimate to final walkthrough.

Is there a fee for a consultation or estimate?

No. Carrillo Roofing LLC offers complimentary estimates to prospective clients.
